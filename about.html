Where it began

One beautiful bag.

KALUKI was built around a deceptively simple idea: one extraordinary bag in two sizes, offered in the most beautiful fabrics we could find, finished with the kind of gold hardware that makes you feel like you've arrived somewhere.

The signature drawstring silhouette is structured enough to stand on its own, compact enough to take everywhere, and beautiful enough that you'll reach for it every single day.

The symbol

The Lucky Elephant.

In many cultures, the elephant represents wisdom, strength, and good fortune. Ours hangs from the tassel of every KALUKI bag — a small reminder that good things go to those who carry them.

Cast in polished gold-finish metal, the lucky elephant is the detail that ties everything together. It is also what KALUKI is named after — the spirit of going further, carrying more, doing it beautifully.
